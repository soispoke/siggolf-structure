# sig.golf, record by record

Every record on the [sig.golf](https://sig.golf) full track, drawn as the structure of the signature: the hypertree, its WOTS+C one-time keys, and the few-time signature at the bottom.

**View it:** https://soispoke.github.io/siggolf-structure/

Space pauses, the arrow keys step through records, and clicking the score line jumps to a record.
