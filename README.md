# sig.golf, record by record

Every record on the [sig.golf](https://sig.golf) full track, drawn as the structure of the signature: the hypertree, its WOTS+C one-time keys, and the few-time signature at the bottom.

- **Records:** https://soispoke.github.io/siggolf-structure/
- **How it works (3 min):** https://soispoke.github.io/siggolf-structure/how-it-works.html

On the records page, space pauses, the arrow keys step through records, and clicking the score line jumps to a record. The explainer is rendered with [Manim](https://www.manim.community).
